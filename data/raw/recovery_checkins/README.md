# Synthetic recovery check-ins

10 fictional patient IDs x 14 consecutive days = 140 rows. All values are simulated; no real patient information is included. The symptom patterns are demonstration scenarios for UI, trend-chart and alert-plumbing tests only. `clinical_status` is always `not_assessed`. These records are not clinical labels and must not be used to train or claim performance of a clinical risk model. A blank temperature means no measurement was simulated. Medication and activity fields are simulated self-reports.
